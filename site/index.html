What's new

The latest data to land on the site, newest first, and what each number means.

  1. Nov 2026Updated Nov 17

    Freight costs

    $14,837 +8.2% y/y

    Shipping a groceries trailer from Tacoma to Anchorage costs $14,837, up 8.2% from a year earlier. The site's all-mode Alaska freight index is up 18.4% over the same span. Freight is built into the shelf price of most goods in the state.

  2. Nov 2026Updated Nov 17

    Job postings

    155 +1.5% y/y

    Indeed job postings in Alaska are at 155% of their February 2020 level, up 1.5% from a year earlier; nationally the index is 104. Postings move ahead of hiring, so they hint at where payroll jobs are heading.

  3. Sep 2026Updated Nov 17

    Asia air freight prices

    +22.0% BLS price index, y/y

    The price of air freight from Asia to the U.S. is up 22.0% from a year earlier. Much of that freight stops at Anchorage, so the price is a gauge of demand for ANC's transpacific cargo business.

  4. Q1 2026Updated Nov 17

    Weekly wages (QCEW)

    $1,519 +4.8% y/y

    The average job in Alaska paid $1,519 a week in Q1 2026, up 4.8% from a year earlier. Mining, oil and gas paid the most among industries BLS reports ($4,418).

  5. Dec 2025Updated Nov 17

    Openings per unemployed

    0.91 JOLTS / LAUS

    Alaska had 16,000 open jobs and 17,531 unemployed people in Dec 2025: 0.9 openings per job seeker. Fewer openings than job seekers means a looser job market.

  6. 2025Updated Nov 17

    Net migration

    −1,740 year to Jul 2025

    1,740 more people left Alaska than moved in during the year to July 2025, the 13th straight year of net out-migration. Births minus deaths (+3,389) more than made up for it, so the population still grew.

  7. 2023Updated Nov 16

    Firm entry rate

    8.2% −0.9 pts vs U.S.

    New employer firms were 8.2% of all Alaska firms in 2023, below the U.S. rate of 9.1%. A higher entry rate means more new competitors and more turnover.

  8. Aug 2026Updated Nov 15

    Natural gas (city gate)

    $10.61 −3.5% y/y

    Gas utilities in Alaska paid $10.61 per thousand cubic feet at the city gate, down 3.5% from a year earlier. The U.S. city gate price was $5.03. The city gate price feeds through to home heating and power bills.

  9. Oct 2026Updated Nov 14

    Business applications

    12,308 +43.2% YTD

    Alaskans filed 12,308 applications to start businesses through October, 43.2% more than a year earlier. Nationally, filings are up 10.9%. Applications are an early read on the firms that will open over the next year or two.

  10. Sep 2026Updated Nov 14

    Wage growth

    +0.7% $38.43/hr

    Average hourly pay for private-sector workers is up 0.7% from a year earlier, to $38.43. After Urban Alaska inflation, real pay was down 1.8% in Aug 2026, so paychecks are losing buying power.
