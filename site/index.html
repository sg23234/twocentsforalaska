Lead story · New business

Alaska leads the country in new business filings

Alaskans filed 11,351 applications to start a business from January through September, up 48.1% from a year earlier and more than in any full year since at least 2018. No other state's filings grew as fast. Applications are intent rather than openings, and a smaller share than nationally look likely to hire.

GDP

Output fell for a second straight quarter

Real GDP shrank at a 1.3% annual rate in the second quarter while the U.S. grew 2.2%. The first quarter was revised down to −3.9%.

Prices

Energy is doing most of the damage

Urban Alaska prices are up 3.3%. Gasoline rose more than a quarter, and rural heating fuel hit a record $7.97 a gallon.

Oil

Crude is far above the state's forecast

Alaska North Slope crude traded near $128 on Oct 21, against a $75 spring forecast for FY2027. The next revenue forecast comes in December.
