Population & migration

The read

Alaska is growing on births alone. More people have left than arrived for 13 straight years, and the gap is now closed only by a shrinking natural increase. The state is getting older fast, while the working-age population has stopped growing. Within the state, people are moving toward the Mat-Su and Kenai and away from rural regions and the older cities.

How Alaska compares

From 2015 to 2025, births minus deaths added about 0.6% a year to Alaska's population, the second-highest rate among states after Utah. Net migration subtracted about the same, the lowest rate of any state.

Twenty states have had more deaths than births since 2020. Alaska is not one of them yet, but its natural increase has fallen by half in a decade.
