Railbelt natural gas

Draft · figures updated by hand

Southcentral Alaska heats homes and generates most of its electricity with natural gas from Cook Inlet. As older fields decline, what utilities pay for gas, and whether they can get enough of it, is one of the biggest input costs for Railbelt households and businesses.
