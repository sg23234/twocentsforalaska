New business formation

Applications through Sep 2026 · Census releases monthly, mid-month · draft
The read

Alaskans are filing for new businesses at the fastest pace since at least 2018. Applications through September already exceed every full year from 2018 to 2025, and Alaska's growth this year is the fastest of any state. Applications are intent, not openings, and a smaller share of Alaska's filings look likely to hire than nationally, though in past years more of them did go on to become employers.

How Alaska compares

Business applications are rising nationwide, with January–August 2026 the highest on record for the U.S. Alaska's growth rate is almost three times the national rate, but per resident it ranks mid-pack, 14th of 50 states and DC.

Data notes

  • An application is a filing for an employer ID number. It is not an LLC registration, an opening, or a job. Many never hire anyone.
  • The monthly series is not seasonally adjusted. Census also publishes a seasonally adjusted version and a weekly series, which a live version of this page would use.
  • The "became employers" figures come from 2022 applications, the latest cohort with a full year of follow-up.
