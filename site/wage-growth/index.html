Pay growth

Real wage growth

Pay growth after inflation: the change in average hourly earnings divided by the change in Urban Alaska consumer prices. Above zero means paychecks buy more than a year earlier.

Weekly wages by industry and place

From the Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages, a count of nearly every job covered by unemployment insurance. Weekly wages mix pay rates with hours, so seasonal and part-time work pulls an industry's average down.

Data notes

  • State earnings estimates come from a sample and move around from month to month; the year-over-year change is more reliable than any single month.
  • Real wage growth uses the Urban Alaska CPI (Anchorage and the Mat-Su), which is the only Alaska price index.
  • QCEW counts jobs by where they are, so North Slope oil field wages show up in the North Slope Borough even though most of those workers live elsewhere.
