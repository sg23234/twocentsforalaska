The read

Alaska home prices rose faster than in any other state over the past year, and mortgage rates are climbing again. Rents are rising more slowly, with the statewide increase the smallest in five years, but the Mat-Su and Kenai are the exceptions, with double-digit gains and tight vacancy. Builders finished more units in 2025 than in any year of the past decade.

How Alaska compares

Alaska led the country in home price growth over the year to Q2 2026, at four times the national rate. Rents are a different picture: Anchorage's median two-bedroom apartment rent of $1,720 is above the U.S. median rent of $1,413, but rent growth across most of the state has slowed.

Data notes
