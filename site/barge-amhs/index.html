Ferry traffic

Passengers and vehicles carried by the Alaska Marine Highway System each calendar year. Ridership was above 300,000 a year through 2014, fell steadily as the fleet aged and sailings were cut, collapsed in 2020, and has recovered to a little over half its 2000s level.

Where the ferries run

Southeast routes carry about three-quarters of passengers; Southwest covers Kodiak, the Kenai Peninsula, Prince William Sound and the Aleutian chain.

Service gaps

  • No cross-Gulf trips ran in 2024 or 2025 because of crew shortages and vessel availability.
  • The Matanuska had no sailings in 2023 and 2024.
  • The Kennicott was out of service for 10 months in 2025 for new generators.

Still to add

  • Barge freight to coastal and river communities. Carriers don't publish volumes, and Army Corps harbor counts for recent years aren't loaded yet.

Data notes

  • Traffic counts are calendar-year totals of passengers and vehicles boarding AMHS vessels, including contract vessels.
