Current account

Draft · data in development

Alaska is a small open economy: most of what it consumes is shipped in and most of what it produces is sold outside the state. A state current account adds up what Alaska earns from the rest of the world and what it pays out, borrowing the framework countries use for their balance of payments. No official statistic does this for a state.

How it works

Current account = goods balance + services balance + primary income balance + transfers balance.

A surplus means Alaska earns more from outside the state than it pays out; a deficit means spending is financed by inflows such as federal transfers, Permanent Fund returns or borrowing.
