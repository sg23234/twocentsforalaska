The read

Alaska students score near the bottom of the country on the national test, and scores have fallen over the past decade. In 2024, Alaska ranked 51st of 53 jurisdictions in three of four national test categories. About 28% of students met the state's own proficiency standard in reading and math. Fewer than four in five high schoolers graduate in four years, and more than two in five students were chronically absent. The state spends about $21,800 per student.

How Alaska compares

Alaska's 4th-grade reading score was 12 points below the national public average in 2024, and its 8th-grade math score 8 points below. In a U.S. News compilation of 2024–25 state graduation rates, Alaska and the District of Columbia were the only places below 80%. Alaska spends about $21,800 per student, and the cost of running schools in remote communities is far higher than in cities.
