Micro · Input costs

Electricity

Shows
Average retail price of electricity in Alaska, all customer sectors combined, in cents per kilowatt-hour, and the percent change from the same month a year earlier.
Source
U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), Electric Power Monthly, retail sales of electricity by state and sector. Retrieved through the EIA Open Data API (electricity/retail-sales).
Series
State: Alaska (AK). Sector: all sectors (ALL). Measure: average price.
Method
The price is revenue divided by kilowatt-hours sold, as reported by utilities to EIA. The change compares the latest month with the same month one year earlier, which avoids seasonal swings.
Updates
Monthly, about two months after the month it covers. This site checks for new data on the 2nd and 16th of each month.
Notes
A statewide average. It blends lower Railbelt and Southeast hydro rates with much higher rates in rural communities, and EIA coverage of the smallest rural utilities is limited. Prices are before Power Cost Equalization credits.

Electricity page: utilities and regions

Shows
Average price per kWh for each Alaska utility and for groups of utilities by region, how each compares with the U.S. average, the change from the year before, and a monthly bill estimate.
Source
U.S. Energy Information Administration, Form EIA-861 Annual Electric Power Industry Report: Sales to Ultimate Customers (long form) and the Short Form (EIA-861S) for small utilities. Hawaii and Washington averages come from the same files.
Method
Price = revenue ÷ kWh sold, by customer type and for all customers. Region averages are weighted by kWh sold. Regions are Two Cents for Alaska's own grouping, loosely following Alaska Energy Authority energy regions, and are listed in data/manual/electricity-regions.json. Regions are ordered by customers served. In the table, utilities with fewer than 1,000 customers are grouped into one row per region, priced by kWh-weighted average; they are still counted in region averages. The bill estimate multiplies monthly use by the utility's average rate for that customer type.
U.S. average
One figure is used for each year across the page: the U.S. all-sector average price from EIA's monthly data, averaged over the year weighted by kWh sold (revenue ÷ kWh, EIA's own method). U.S. averages computed from EIA-861 count only the energy-supplier row for customers in retail-choice states. The delivery utility's row repeats the same kWh and is counted for revenue only, so electricity is not counted twice.
Excluded
Napakiak Ircinraq Power Co. Its 2025 EIA-861 figures (214¢ per kWh, up 143% from 2024) look like a reporting error. Exclusions are listed in data/manual/electricity-regions.json.
Updates
Annual. EIA publishes final data for a year in the fall of the following year; this site picks up a new year automatically.
Notes
Covers utilities that report to EIA. Some of the smallest village utilities report only to the state's Power Cost Equalization program and are not included. Prices are before Power Cost Equalization credits.

Electricity page: Power Cost Equalization

Shows
How the state's Power Cost Equalization (PCE) credit is set, program totals, and an estimate of what a village household pays after the credit.
Source
Alaska Energy Authority, Power Cost Equalization program materials (program rules; FY2025: $46 million, 188 communities, 81,000+ Alaskans). Base rate from the Regulatory Commission of Alaska: 22.11¢ per kWh for FY2027 (bills from July 1, 2026), up from 20.38¢ for FY2026.
Per person
About $570 a year = $46 million paid in FY2025 ÷ 81,000 Alaskans who benefit, both from AEA. Includes the credit on community buildings.
Method
Credit per kWh = 95% × (cost − base rate), with cost capped at $1.00, on a home's first 750 kWh a month. The estimate uses each utility's average residential price from EIA-861 as its cost, or the all-customer price where no residential figure is reported.
Updates
By hand when the base rate changes (each July) or new program totals are published.
Notes
Estimates only. Actual PCE levels are set for each community from the utility's own costs and can be lower, and payments are cut in proportion when fund earnings fall short.

Electricity page: household comparison

Shows
Monthly household bill at the average residential price per kWh for selected Alaska places, Hawaii, Washington, the U.S., Iceland, Norway and British Columbia.
Sources
Alaska, Hawaii, Washington and U.S.: EIA-861 residential averages. Norway: Statistics Norway, 2025 household price including grid rent and taxes, before state support (141.7 øre/kWh). Iceland: GlobalPetrolPrices, March 2025, all taxes and fees (USD 0.201/kWh). British Columbia: BC Utilities Commission, BC Hydro residential rate from April 2025 (basic charge, step 1 and step 2), at 750 kWh a month, with 5% GST. Exchange rates: Federal Reserve G.5A 2025 averages (NOK 10.3852, CAD 1.3973 per USD).
Updates
U.S. figures update automatically each year. The figures for Iceland, Norway and British Columbia are entered by hand in the page and need a yearly update.
Notes
U.S. figures exclude sales tax; the figures outside the U.S. include taxes. Alaska figures are before PCE credits.
