Freight rate index

Live data

Almost everything sold in Alaska arrives by ship, barge, truck or plane, so shipping costs are built into the price of nearly every good. No public index tracked what it costs to move freight to and within Alaska, so this page builds one from the carriers' own published rates and fuel surcharges.

The index

Monthly, Jan 2025 = 100. The Lower 48 benchmark weights national producer price indexes for ocean, rail, truck and air freight the same way.

What a shipment costs now

The latest published rate for each route the index tracks, with the date it took effect. Fuel surcharges are added on top of the base rates.

Fuel surcharges

Carriers adjust these weekly or monthly as fuel prices move. They explain most of the month-to-month change in freight costs.

Air freight to rural hubs

Northern Air Cargo's general rate per pound from Anchorage, 1,000 lb shipments, before fuel surcharge. Earlier years come from archived copies of its rates page.

How the index is built

Each part is a delivered price: base rate times one plus the fuel surcharge, plus fixed charges. Ocean is TOTE's Tacoma–Anchorage truckload rate for groceries with TOTE's ocean fuel surcharge and the Port of Alaska modernization surcharge. Rail and truck move with their fuel surcharges. Last mile is the average of Northern Air Cargo's and Everts' 1,000 lb rates to Bethel, Nome and Kotzebue with the in-state air fuel surcharge.

Every rate and surcharge the collector sees is saved with the date it took effect, so the index can be rebuilt and checked. Weights and the base month are set in data/manual/freight.json.

These are published list prices. Large shippers negotiate contract rates, which move with the same surcharges but start from different base rates.

Data notes

  • Rail and truck base rates are held constant; only their fuel surcharges move. The Alaska Railroad and Carlile do not post their base-rate history.
  • The in-state air surcharge comes from Carlile Transportation's posted history, used as a stand-in because Northern Air Cargo and Everts post only their current surcharge. Their own current surcharges appear in the table above.
  • TOTE's ocean surcharge did not change between May 2022 and March 2026, so the level in its oldest posted tariff is used for that whole stretch.
  • TOTE posts only recent tariffs. Before November 2026 its rate is carried back using its general rate increases: 6.25% (Nov 2026), 5.75% (Nov 2025) and 7.5% (Nov 2024).
  • Carlile's in-state air surcharge was last posted for Sep 15, 2026; its last rate (58%) is carried forward.
  • Carlile's Southeast barge surcharge was last posted for Sep 27, 2026; its last rate (17%) is carried forward.
  • Carlile's Central Alaska barge surcharge was last posted for Sep 22, 2026; its last rate (32%) is carried forward.
